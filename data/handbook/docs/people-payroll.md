# Payroll and payslips
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Payday

Salaries are paid by bank transfer on the last working day of each month. If the last day of the month is a weekend or a public holiday, you are paid on the working day before. Your first salary is paid on the first payday after you start, for the days you worked.

## Payslips

Your payslip is in PeopleHub 3 working days before payday. It shows your salary, allowances, expenses repaid, overtime, deductions for tax, your retirement plan contribution and any other deductions. Download your payslips for your records, because PeopleHub keeps them for 7 years only.

## Changes to your details

Change your bank details, address or tax details in PeopleHub by the 15th of the month for the change to apply to that month's pay. Changes after the 15th apply the next month. For your security, a change of bank details is confirmed by a message to your phone, and the People team will never ask you to change bank details by email.

## What is paid with salary

- Your monthly salary.
- Allowances, such as the internet allowance and the on-call allowance.
- Overtime approved by the 15th of the month.
- Expense claims approved by the 20th of the month.

## Mistakes in your pay

If your pay looks wrong, tell the People team within 30 days of payday, at people@fernhill.example. An underpayment is corrected within 5 working days, by a separate transfer. An overpayment is repaid by agreement, usually from the next month's pay or spread over up to 3 months.

## Salary advances

In an emergency, you may ask for a salary advance of up to half of one month's net salary, once in any 12 months. It is repaid from your pay over the next 3 months, with no interest.

## Tax documents

Your yearly tax statement is in PeopleHub by 15 June each year. Submit your tax-saving declarations in PeopleHub during April, so the right amount of tax is deducted through the year.

## Final pay when you leave

Your final pay is paid on the normal payday after your last day. It includes unused annual leave and any expenses approved by then. Amounts you owe, such as leave taken beyond what you earned or equipment not returned, are deducted.
