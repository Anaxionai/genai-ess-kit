# Client meals and team meals
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to everyone who pays for a meal with clients, partners or colleagues and claims it back. Meals on business trips for yourself alone are covered by the daily meal allowance in the hotels and meals policy, not by this page.

## Client meals and client dinners

The limit for a client lunch or a client dinner is ₹3,000 per person, including drinks, taxes and the tip.

The limit applies to everyone at the table, Fernhill employees and guests alike. A client dinner where the total bill will be over ₹25,000 needs your department head's approval before it happens. Alcohol is allowed at client meals, in reasonable amounts. In the claim, list the Fernhill employees who attended and the name of the client's company. Do not list the guests' names. Attach the itemised receipt, not just the card slip.

## Who pays

The most senior Fernhill employee at the meal pays and claims it. This means a manager does not approve their own team's meal with a guest they invited. If two departments share a client meal, the cost is split by agreement and each half is claimed by one person.

## Team meals

Each team may hold 2 team meals a year, paid by the company, up to ₹1,500 per person each time. The team's manager pays and claims it. Team meals celebrate something, such as a release or a team member's farewell. Alcohol at team meals is a personal cost; ask the restaurant for a separate bill for drinks.

## Working late

If your manager asks you to work in the office after 20:00, you may claim a meal of up to ₹600 with a receipt. This is not available when you work late at home.

## Gifts for clients

A gift for a client may cost up to ₹2,500, and is given only at the end of a project or at a festival. Gifts of cash or vouchers are never allowed. Record every client gift in the gifts register, as the code of conduct explains.

## Common mistakes

The most common mistakes are a missing itemised receipt, a claim for a meal with colleagues only that is not one of the 2 team meals, and a total above the limit. Claims above the limit are paid only up to the limit, and the rest is a personal cost.
