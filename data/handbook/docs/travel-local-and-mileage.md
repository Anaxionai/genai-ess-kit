# Local travel and mileage
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Public transport and taxis

Use public transport for local business journeys where it is practical. Taxis and ride-hailing are fine when there is no suitable public transport, after 21:00, when you carry heavy equipment, or when a taxi saves at least 30 minutes on a busy day. Claim local fares in ExpenseHub. A receipt is needed only for a fare over ₹500.

## Using your own vehicle

Business journeys in your own car are paid at ₹12 per kilometre. Business journeys on your own motorbike or scooter are paid at ₹6 per kilometre. Your normal commute between home and your office is never a business journey. If you go from home to a client, and the journey is longer than your normal commute, claim only the extra kilometres.

## How to claim mileage

Claim mileage in ExpenseHub with a log for each journey: the date, where you went from and to, the number of kilometres, and the reason for the trip. Use a map service to check the distance. Parking charges and road tolls are paid with receipts.

## What is never paid

Fines for parking, speeding or other traffic offences are never paid by the company. Fuel for your own vehicle is not claimed separately, because the rate per kilometre already covers it. Car servicing, repairs and insurance are personal costs.

## Insurance

Your own vehicle insurance must cover business use. The company does not pay for this cover. If you have an accident on a business journey, tell your manager and the People team the same day.

## Commuting to another office

If your manager asks you to work from the other office for a day, you may claim the extra cost of the journey compared with your normal commute. Regular visits to the other office as part of your hybrid pattern are not business journeys.

## Late working

If you leave the office after 21:00 because of work your manager asked for, you may take a taxi home and claim it, up to ₹1,200.
