# Booking work travel
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Before you book

Travel only when a video call will not do. Every trip needs your manager's approval before anything is booked. Request approval in TripDesk by selecting New trip request, with the dates, the reason and an estimate of the cost. A trip that will cost more than ₹1,50,000 in total also needs your department head's approval. Approval usually takes 2 working days. Check the hotel limits and the daily meal allowance in the hotels and meals policy before you plan the trip. For travel abroad, read the international travel policy too.

## Flights

Flights under six hours are booked in economy class. Business class is allowed for longer flights, of six hours or more, with your manager's approval in TripDesk. Premium economy is not booked. Book flights at least 14 days before you fly. A booking made later than that needs your department head's approval, because late fares cost much more. Choose the cheapest flight that fits your meeting times, and do not pick an airline for its loyalty points.

## Trains and road travel

Trains are booked in standard class, or in the air-conditioned chair car on day journeys. A sleeper berth is allowed for overnight journeys. First class is allowed only if it costs less than standard class on the day. Taxis to and from the airport or station are fine. Car hire needs your manager's approval and is booked through TripDesk.

## How to book

Book flights, trains and hotels in TripDesk. They are paid by the company directly, so you do not claim them as expenses. If TripDesk cannot find a hotel within the limit, ask the travel desk, and do not book outside TripDesk. Your booking confirmation and itinerary arrive by email from TripDesk, usually within an hour.

## Changes and cancellations

Change or cancel a booking in TripDesk as soon as you know your plans have changed. Many fares can be changed free of charge up to 24 hours before departure. If a change will cost more than ₹10,000, your manager must approve it in TripDesk first. Out of office hours, call the travel desk's emergency line shown on your itinerary. A missed flight caused by your own lateness is paid by you, unless your manager agrees otherwise.

## Questions

Email the travel desk at travel@fernhill.example. The travel desk answers within 1 working day, and within 2 hours for travel in the next 48 hours.
