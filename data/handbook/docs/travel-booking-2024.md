# Booking work travel (2024)
Department: Finance
Updated: 2024-04-01

## Status of this document

SUPERSEDED. This document was replaced on 12 January 2026 by the current policy on booking work travel. Do not follow these rules. They are kept for one year so that you can compare the old and new rules.

## Approval

Managers approved trips by email, and the approval email had to be attached to the booking. A trip costing more than ₹1,00,000 in total also needed the department head's approval. Approval had to be given before any booking was made.

## Flights

Flights under eight hours were booked in economy class. Flights of eight hours or more were booked in premium economy. Business class was not allowed for anyone. Flights had to be booked at least 21 days before travel.

## Trains

Trains were booked in standard class only. Sleeper berths were allowed for journeys of more than 8 hours.

## How bookings were made

Bookings were made by email to the travel desk, which replied with options within 2 working days. Employees could book their own hotels on any website and claim the cost in ExpenseHub with a receipt, within the hotel limits of that time.

## Changes

Changes were requested by email to the travel desk. Any change that cost more than ₹5,000 needed the manager's approval.

## Why it changed

The 2025 travel review found that bookings by email were slow and that self-booked hotels often cost more than the limits. TripDesk was introduced in January 2026 for all bookings, the approval threshold was raised, and business class became possible for long flights with a manager's approval.

## Hotels and meals in 2024

Hotels were limited to ₹5,000 a night in most cities and ₹7,500 a night in high-cost cities. The daily meal allowance was ₹1,500 in India and ₹3,500 abroad, and receipts were needed for every meal over ₹300. These limits were raised in January 2026; the current figures are in the hotels and meals policy.
