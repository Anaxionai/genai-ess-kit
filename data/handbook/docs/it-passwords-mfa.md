# Passwords and multi-factor authentication
Department: IT
Updated: 2026-02-02

## Why this matters

Most break-ins start with a stolen or guessed password. Strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication stop nearly all of them. This policy applies to every company account, and to any personal account you use for work.

## Password rules

- A password must have at least 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more random words is easiest to remember.
- Never reuse a company password anywhere else, including other company systems.
- Never share a password, not even with the IT service desk. IT staff will never ask for it.
- Do not write passwords on paper or in a text file.

You do not have to change your password on a schedule. Change it at once if you think someone else may know it, and report it as a security incident.

## The password manager

Every laptop has the company password manager. Store all work passwords in it. It creates strong passwords for you and fills them in. Its own master password must be a long passphrase that you use nowhere else. If you forget the master password, the IT service desk can reset your vault after checking your identity by video call.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication, often called MFA, means a second check after your password. It is required on every company account. Use the authenticator app on your phone. Text message codes are not allowed, because they can be stolen. If you get a new phone, move the authenticator app before you wipe the old one. If you lose your phone, call the IT service desk on extension 4400 to reset your MFA.

## MFA fatigue attacks

If your phone asks you to approve a sign-in you did not start, always deny it. Then report it to security@fernhill.example. Attackers send many prompts in the hope that you approve one by mistake.

## Shared accounts

Shared accounts are not allowed, except for a few service accounts that IT manages. Each service account has an owner who reviews it every 6 months.

## Getting help

Locked out of your account? Call the IT service desk on extension 4400, or use the self-service reset page from another device. The service desk is open from 08:00 to 20:00 on working days. Outside those hours, the on-call IT engineer handles urgent lock-outs.
