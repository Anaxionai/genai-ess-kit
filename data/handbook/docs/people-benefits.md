# Benefits
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Health insurance

All employees have health insurance from their first day. The policy covers you, your partner, your children and up to 2 parents or parents-in-law. The cover is ₹10,00,000 a year for the whole family, for hospital treatment. Outpatient visits, such as a doctor's consultation, are covered up to ₹15,000 a year per family. Add or change the people covered in PeopleHub within 30 days of a life event, such as a marriage or a birth, or during January's benefits window.

## Retirement plan

The company runs a retirement plan for every employee. If you put in at least 3% of your salary, the company adds 5%. You may put in more, but the company's share stays at 5%. You can change your own contribution in PeopleHub once a quarter.

## Life cover

If you die while employed by Fernhill Software, the people you name receive 4 times your yearly salary. Name your beneficiaries in PeopleHub and keep the names up to date.

## Employee assistance programme

The employee assistance programme gives free, confidential help 24 hours a day, every day of the year. It offers counselling, with up to 6 sessions a year and more when needed, and advice on money, legal questions and family care. Your call is never reported to the company. The phone number is on the benefits page in PeopleHub.

## Health checks

Employees aged 35 and over may have a free health check once a year at a partner clinic. Employees under 35 may have one every 2 years.

## Cycle to work

You can buy a bicycle and safety equipment through the cycle-to-work scheme, up to ₹40,000. The company pays for the bicycle, and you repay it from your salary over 12 months.

## Benefits window

Every January there is a benefits window in PeopleHub. During it you can change your health insurance cover, buy up to 3 extra days of annual leave, and join the cycle-to-work scheme.

## Part-time employees

Part-time employees get all the same benefits. The retirement plan and life cover are based on their actual salary.

## Leaving

Health insurance ends on your last day. You may keep the employee assistance programme for 3 months after you leave.
