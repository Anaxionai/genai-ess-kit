# Wellbeing days
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## What a wellbeing day is

Every employee gets 2 paid wellbeing days in each calendar year. A wellbeing day is a day away from work to rest and look after your health. You do not need to be ill, and you do not need to give a reason. Wellbeing days are separate from annual leave and from sick leave. They were introduced in 2025 after the staff survey asked for time to recover from busy periods.

## How to book one

Book a wellbeing day in PeopleHub with at least 1 working day's notice. Your manager can only decline it if the day clashes with a commitment that cannot move, such as a release or a client workshop you lead. In that case your manager must offer another day within 2 weeks. Take wellbeing days one at a time. You cannot book both days as a block, and you cannot join a wellbeing day to sick leave.

## Rules

- Wellbeing days do not carry over to the next year.
- They are not paid out when you leave the company.
- A wellbeing day on which you fall ill is not given back as sick leave.
- Part-time employees get wellbeing days pro rata, rounded up to a whole day.
- New starters get both days in their first year, whatever month they join.

## Using the day well

The day is yours. Many people use it for a long walk, a medical check-up, or simply a quiet day at home. Please do not use it to catch up on work, and switch off chat notifications for the day.

## When you need more than a day

If you are struggling for longer than a day or two, talk to your manager or to the People team. The employee assistance programme gives free, confidential counselling, 24 hours a day, with up to 6 sessions a year. The benefits policy explains how to reach it. If you are unwell and cannot work, use sick leave, not a wellbeing day.

## Managers

Managers should remind their teams about wellbeing days after long projects and before the end of the year, and should take their own wellbeing days too.
