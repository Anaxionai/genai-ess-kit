# Learning and development
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Your learning budget

Every permanent employee has a learning budget of ₹60,000 a year, for courses, books, exams and conference tickets. You also get 3 study days a year, on top of your annual leave, for courses, exams or conferences. Unused budget and unused study days do not carry over to the next year. Part-time employees get the full budget and study days pro rata.

## How to use it

First, agree the learning with your manager and record it in PeopleHub. Your manager should approve anything that is relevant to your current role or the role you are working towards. Then either pay for it yourself and claim it in ExpenseHub with the receipt, or ask Finance to pay the provider directly, which is better for anything over ₹15,000.

## Conferences

A conference ticket comes from your learning budget. Travel and hotels for a conference follow the travel policies and are booked through TripDesk. They do not come from your learning budget. Speaking at a conference is encouraged; speakers' travel is paid even if the ticket is free.

## Paying it back

If a single course or exam costs more than ₹25,000 and you leave the company:
- within 6 months of finishing it, you repay 100% of its cost;
- within 12 months of finishing it, you repay 50% of its cost.

You do not repay anything if the company ends your employment, or for courses that cost ₹25,000 or less.

## Learning at work

Every Thursday from 16:00 to 17:00 is learning hour, with no meetings. Use it for courses, reading or practice. Teams run short talks, called lunch and learn sessions, every month.

## Sharing what you learn

After a conference or a course, share three things you learned with your team, in writing or at a team meeting.

## Free resources

The company pays for an online learning library that every employee can use without touching their budget. Ask the People team for access.
