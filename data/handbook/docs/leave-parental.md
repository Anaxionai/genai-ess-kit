# Parental leave
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## What this covers

This policy covers leave when you have a child by birth, adoption or surrogacy. It gives more than the legal minimum. Where the law gives you more in a particular case, the law applies.

## Who qualifies

You qualify for company parental pay if you have worked at Fernhill Software for at least 6 months by the expected week of birth, or by the date a child is placed with you for adoption. If you have worked here for less than 6 months, you still get the leave, with the pay the law gives.

## Primary carer

The primary carer gets 26 weeks of leave at full pay. After that, they may take up to 13 more weeks of unpaid leave. Tell the People team at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth, or within 7 days of being matched with a child for adoption. You may start the leave up to 11 weeks before the expected week of birth.

## Secondary carer

The secondary carer, usually the partner, gets 4 weeks of leave at full pay. Take it in blocks of at least one week, within 12 months of the birth or placement. Tell your manager at least 4 weeks before each block, or as soon as you can if the baby arrives early.

## While you are away

You keep all your benefits during parental leave, including health insurance and the retirement plan. You keep earning annual leave, and the People team agrees with you when to take it. You may work up to 10 keeping-in-touch days during the leave, paid at your normal rate. They are optional, and you agree each one with your manager. Your manager will not contact you about work, except to share news you asked to hear, such as a reorganisation.

## Coming back

You return to the same job on the same terms. If you would like to come back part time or with different hours, ask the People team at least 8 weeks before you return. They answer within 4 weeks. A phased return over 2 weeks, using some of your annual leave, is common and encouraged.

## Other time off for parents

Pregnant employees get paid time off for antenatal appointments. Partners get paid time off for 2 antenatal appointments. Emergency time off to look after a sick child is agreed with your manager. Work-from-anywhere days cannot be added to parental leave.

## Questions

Contact the People team at people@fernhill.example. They will send you a personal plan with your dates and pay once you tell them.
