# Corporate card
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Who gets a corporate card

A corporate card is a company credit card in your name. It is given to employees who travel for work at least 4 times a year, and to employees whose role needs one, such as office managers who buy supplies. Ask Finance for a card through ExpenseHub. Your manager must support the request. The card arrives at your office within 10 working days.

## Limits

The monthly limit is ₹3,00,000. Finance can raise the limit for a planned trip if you ask at least 5 working days before. Cash withdrawals with the card are limited to ₹20,000 a month and only for business costs abroad.

## Rules for using the card

Use the card only for business costs. Personal spending on the card is never allowed, even if you plan to pay it back. Do not share the card or its number with anyone, including colleagues. Do not save the card in personal shopping accounts. Hotels and flights should still be booked through TripDesk, not paid with the card.

## Receipts and matching

Upload the receipt for each card payment to ExpenseHub within 7 days. ExpenseHub shows each card payment as a line waiting for a receipt. Match every payment to a receipt, or to a reason if no receipt exists, by the 5th working day of the next month. Unmatched payments after that date are reported to your manager. Three months in a row with unmatched payments means the card is cancelled.

## Lost or stolen card

If the card is lost or stolen, call the bank on the number printed in ExpenseHub at once, so the card is blocked. Then tell Finance at finance@fernhill.example within 24 hours. A new card arrives within 5 working days. You are not responsible for fraudulent payments if you report the loss on time.

## Leaving the company

Return the card to Finance, or cut it in half and send a photo, on your last day. Match your final payments before you go. Any personal spending found later is deducted from your final pay.

## Mistakes

If you use the card for a personal cost by mistake, tell Finance the same day and repay it by bank transfer within 7 days. A second mistake in 12 months means the card is cancelled.
