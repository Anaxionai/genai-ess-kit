# Hotels and the daily meal allowance
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Hotel limits

Book hotels through TripDesk, within these limits for each night:
- ₹6,000 a night in most cities in India.
- ₹9,000 a night in high-cost cities. TripDesk shows which cities count as high-cost.
- ₹15,000 a night for hotels abroad.

The limits include taxes and breakfast. Book a standard room. If no hotel within the limit is available near your meeting, the travel desk can approve a higher rate before you book.

## What the company pays at the hotel

The room, taxes, breakfast if included, and internet access are paid by the company. Laundry is paid only on trips of 5 nights or more. Minibar items, films, spa services and room upgrades are personal costs, and you pay them when you check out.

## The daily meal allowance

On a business trip that includes at least one night away, you get a daily meal allowance instead of claiming each meal:
- ₹1,800 a day for travel in India.
- ₹4,500 a day for travel abroad.

The allowance covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. You do not need receipts for it. Claim it in ExpenseHub as one line per day. If your hotel includes breakfast, the allowance is reduced by ₹400 for that day. On the day you travel home, you get half the allowance if you arrive home before 14:00.

## Day trips

On a trip with no night away, there is no daily allowance. You may claim one meal of up to ₹600 with a receipt if you are away for more than 8 hours.

## Client meals on a trip

Meals with clients are not paid from the daily allowance. They follow the client meals policy, and you claim them separately with a receipt.

## Staying with friends or family

If you stay with friends or family instead of a hotel, you may claim ₹1,500 a night for a small gift, without a receipt.
