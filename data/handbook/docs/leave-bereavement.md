# Bereavement and compassionate leave
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Bereavement leave

When someone close to you dies, take the time you need. The company gives 5 days of paid bereavement leave when a close family member dies. A close family member is a partner, a child, a parent, a sibling, or a person who brought you up. For another relative, or a close friend, the company gives 2 days of paid bereavement leave. You do not need to take the days in one block. Use them within 3 months, for example for the funeral and for days afterwards. You do not need to provide any proof.

## Telling us

Tell your manager as soon as you feel able, by phone, chat or message. If that is too hard, ask a colleague or a family member to call the People team on extension 4300. Your manager records the leave in PeopleHub for you.

## Compassionate leave

You may take up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave a year to care for a close family member who is seriously ill, or to support them through treatment. More time can be agreed as unpaid leave or as annual leave. Your manager and the People team will also consider flexible hours or working from home for a period.

## Pregnancy loss

If you or your partner lose a pregnancy, you are entitled to 10 days of paid leave. This leave is in addition to any sick leave you need. You do not need to give details.

## Coming back

Your manager can agree lighter duties for a few weeks after you return. Grief can return at unexpected times, so tell your manager if you need a short break later. Nobody will ask you to explain.

## Support

Free, confidential counselling is available from the employee assistance programme, 24 hours a day, with up to 6 sessions a year and more when needed. The benefits policy explains how to reach it. The People team can help with practical matters, such as changes to your benefits or your beneficiary details.

## Managers

Managers should contact the employee once, kindly, to ask what they would like colleagues to be told. Do not ask for proof, and do not count bereavement leave in any absence figures.
