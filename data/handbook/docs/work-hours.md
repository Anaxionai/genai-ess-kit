# Working hours
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Standard hours

A full-time working week at Fernhill Software is 37.5 hours, normally Monday to Friday. Your contract states your hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. During core hours, be available for meetings and messages unless you are on leave or your manager has agreed otherwise. Outside core hours you choose when to work, as long as your team's work is covered.

## Breaks

Take a lunch break of 30 to 60 minutes each day. The lunch break is unpaid and is not counted in the 37.5 hours. Take short breaks away from the screen every hour or so. If you work more than 6 hours in a day, a break of at least 20 minutes is required.

## Part-time work

Part-time employees agree their hours and days with their manager in writing, and PeopleHub records them. Annual leave, wellbeing days and allowances are pro rata for part-time employees, unless a policy says otherwise. Anyone may ask to work part time. The People team replies to a request within 4 weeks.

## Recording time

You do not record your hours unless you work overtime or are on the on-call rota. The overtime and on-call policy explains how to record those hours. Do not regularly work more than 48 hours a week. If your workload needs it, talk to your manager, who must find a solution within 2 weeks.

## Meetings

Avoid booking meetings before 10:00 or after 16:00 for colleagues in another office or working from home. Friday afternoons after 13:00 are meeting-free, for focused work. Keep meetings to 25 or 50 minutes so people have time between them. Every meeting invitation should say its purpose in one sentence.

## Being late or leaving early

If you cannot start at your usual time, tell your team in chat. Occasional late starts are made up during the week. Regular changes to your hours need your manager's agreement.

## Questions

Ask your manager, or the People team at people@fernhill.example.
