# International travel
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Before you go

Register every trip abroad in TripDesk at least 10 working days before you leave. Registration turns on the company's travel insurance for you automatically, and the policy number appears on your itinerary. Check that your passport is valid for at least 6 months after your return date. Visa fees and required vaccinations are paid by the company. Claim them in ExpenseHub with receipts.

## High-risk countries

The Security team keeps a list of high-risk countries in TripDesk. For a country on that list, the Security team must approve the trip at least 10 working days before you travel. They will brief you on local risks and may ask you to check in by message every day.

## Devices and data

Take only your company laptop and phone, with full-disk encryption turned on. The IT service desk can check this for you before you go. Do not carry Restricted data on USB drives or on paper. Use the VPN on every network. Some countries may inspect devices at the border. If asked, comply calmly, then report it to security@fernhill.example within 24 hours, because the laptop may need to be checked.

## Money abroad

The daily meal allowance abroad is ₹4,500, and hotels abroad may cost up to ₹15,000 a night. Card payments in another currency are claimed at the rate shown on your card statement. Cash withdrawals for business costs are claimed with the bank's receipt. The company does not pay foreign transaction fees on a personal card if a corporate card was available to you.

## Health and safety

The travel insurance covers medical treatment, lost luggage and travel delays. Keep the assistance number from your insurance card in your phone. In an emergency, call the assistance number first, then tell your manager. If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, follow the lost devices policy at once.

## Working hours abroad

Long flights are tiring. After an overnight flight you may start work at noon the next day. Travel time on a weekend gives time off in lieu, hour for hour, up to 8 hours per trip.
