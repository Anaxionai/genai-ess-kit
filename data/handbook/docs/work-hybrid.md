# Hybrid working
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to employees in office-based roles at the Riverside and Hilltop offices. It replaces the remote work guidelines of 2024. Employees hired into fully remote roles follow their contract instead, and the rules on days in the office do not apply to them.

## Days in the office

Spend at least 2 days a week in your office. One of the 2 days is your team's anchor day, when the whole team is in. Your manager chooses the anchor day with the team and records it in DeskBook. On the other days you may work from home or from the office. In a week with a public holiday or your own leave, 1 day in the office is enough.

## Booking a desk

Book a desk in DeskBook before you come in. Check in at the desk by 11:00, or the booking is released for someone else. Teams have neighbourhoods of desks on anchor days, so you can sit together. Leave the desk clear at the end of the day.

## Working from home

Work from a quiet, private place where your screen cannot be overlooked. Use the company VPN for anything classified Confidential or Restricted. Be reachable by chat and phone during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00. Lock your laptop whenever you step away, even at home.

## Allowances

Employees on the hybrid pattern receive an internet allowance of ₹1,500 a month, paid with salary. The one-off home office equipment allowance and the other allowances are described in the allowances policy.

## Working from another place

Working from somewhere other than your home or office, including another country, is covered by the work from anywhere policy.

## Exceptions

If you need a different pattern for health or caring reasons, ask your manager and the People team. Adjustments are agreed case by case and reviewed every 6 months. Managers may ask a team to come in on extra days for a planned event, with at least 1 week's notice.
