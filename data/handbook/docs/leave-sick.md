# Sick leave
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Telling us you are ill

If you are too ill to work, tell your manager by phone or chat by 10:00 on the first day. An email alone is not enough, because it may not be read in time. If you cannot contact your manager, call the People team on extension 4300. Record the absence in PeopleHub as soon as you are able. If you are off for more than 2 days, update your manager every 2 or 3 days.

## Self-certification and doctor's notes

For an absence of up to 3 consecutive working days you self-certify. That means you record the absence and its reason in PeopleHub, and no doctor's note is needed. From the 4th working day of an absence you need a doctor's note. Upload a photo of the note to PeopleHub. If an absence goes on for longer than the note covers, send a new note.

## Sick pay

You get full pay for up to 10 working days of sick leave in any rolling 12 months. After that you get half pay for up to 20 more working days. After 30 working days of sick leave in 12 months, the People team agrees support and pay with you case by case. The rolling 12 months means the 12 months before each day of sickness, not the calendar year. Sick pay is paid with your normal salary on the last working day of the month.

## Coming back to work

After an absence of 5 or more working days, you and your manager have a short return-to-work conversation in your first week back. It is a chance to agree any support you need, not a disciplinary meeting. A phased return, with shorter days for a few weeks, can be agreed with the People team after a long absence. Reasonable changes to your work, such as a different chair or fewer early meetings, can be agreed too.

## Sickness during annual leave

If you fall ill on annual leave, tell your manager on the first day and send a doctor's note. Those days become sick leave and your annual leave is given back.

## What sick leave is not for

Sick leave is for your own illness or injury. Caring for a sick child or partner is covered by emergency time off for dependants, agreed with your manager, or by compassionate leave. Wellbeing days are separate from sick leave, and a wellbeing day on which you fall ill is not given back.

## Your privacy

Medical details you share are kept by the People team only. Your manager is told how long you are likely to be away and whether you need changes at work, not your diagnosis.
