# Home office and monthly allowances
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## Home office equipment

Employees on the hybrid pattern and fully remote employees may claim a one-off home office equipment allowance of ₹25,000. Use it for a chair, a desk, a monitor, a headset or similar items that help you work safely at home. The allowance can be claimed once every 4 years. Claim it in ExpenseHub with receipts. The items you buy stay yours, also when you leave. You may also borrow a company monitor and keyboard instead. Ask the IT service desk, and return them when you leave.

## Internet allowance

Employees on the hybrid pattern and fully remote employees receive an internet allowance of ₹1,500 a month. It is paid with salary and does not need a claim or a receipt. It starts in the month after you join and stops when you move to a pattern that does not qualify.

## Mobile allowance

Employees on the on-call rota who use their own phone receive a mobile allowance of ₹1,000 a month. Employees who prefer a company phone get one instead of the allowance. Other employees do not get a mobile allowance, because work calls use the laptop.

## Taxes

The internet and mobile allowances are paid as part of your salary, so they are taxed as income. The one-off equipment allowance repays a real cost, so it is not taxed.

## What is not covered

Electricity, heating, cooling, rent and furniture beyond the one-off allowance are not paid. Printer ink and paper are not paid, because printing at home should be rare. Snacks and drinks at home are personal costs.

## Health and safety at home

Set up your desk so that the top of the screen is at eye level, your feet rest flat, and your elbows are at desk height. If you have pain when you work, ask the People team for a free workstation check by video. If the check recommends equipment beyond the allowance, the company pays for it.

## Changes

These amounts are reviewed every January. The People team announces any change in December.
