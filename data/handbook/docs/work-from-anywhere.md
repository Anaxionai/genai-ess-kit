# Work from anywhere
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## What you can do

You may work from somewhere other than your home or office, including another country, for up to 20 working days in each calendar year. This policy applies to permanent employees who have passed probation.

## How to ask

Request it in PeopleHub at least 15 working days before you go. Your manager and the People team must both approve it. Say where you will be and which dates. If plans change, update the request before you travel.

## Where you can go

Working from another country is allowed only if the country is on the approved country list in PeopleHub. The list exists because of tax and immigration rules, and it changes, so check it each time. Working from anywhere in your home country is always allowed.

## Rules while you are away

- You must be online for at least 3 hours of your home office's core hours, 10:00 to 16:00, each day.
- The 20 days are counted per calendar year and do not carry over.
- Days abroad on business travel do not count toward the 20 days.
- Work-from-anywhere days cannot be added to parental leave.
- You keep your normal hours, deadlines and on-call duties, unless your manager agrees a change.

## Security

Use only your company laptop, with the VPN on at all times. Never work on Restricted data over public Wi-Fi, even with the VPN. Do not leave your laptop in a car or an unattended hotel room. If your laptop is lost or stolen while you are away, follow the lost devices policy at once and report it within 1 hour.

## Costs

You pay your own travel, accommodation and any visa costs. They are not business expenses and cannot be claimed in ExpenseHub. The company's travel insurance does not cover work-from-anywhere trips, so arrange your own.

## If something goes wrong

If you cannot work because of a connection problem, tell your manager and take annual leave for the lost time, or make it up later in the week.
