# Overtime, time off in lieu and on-call
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Paid overtime for grades 1 to 3

Employees in grades 1 to 3 are paid for overtime. Hours beyond 37.5 a week are paid at 1.5 times your hourly rate on weekdays and Saturdays. Hours on a Sunday or a public holiday are paid at 2 times your hourly rate. Overtime must be approved in PeopleHub before you work it. Overtime that was not approved in advance is not paid. Overtime approved by the 15th of the month is paid with that month's salary.

## Time off in lieu for grade 4 and above

Employees in grade 4 and above are not paid for overtime. Instead they get time off in lieu, hour for hour, for extra hours their manager asked them to work. Use time off in lieu within 3 months, or it is lost. You may hold at most 5 days of time off in lieu at any time. Record it in PeopleHub.

## The on-call rota

Engineering and IT run an on-call rota. On-call weeks run from Monday 09:00 to the next Monday 09:00. Nobody is on call for more than 1 week in every 4. Swaps are allowed if you record them in the rota tool at least 24 hours ahead.

## What on-call means

When you are on call, answer an alert within 15 minutes, day or night. Stay within reach of your laptop and a reliable internet connection. Write a short note for every call-out in the incident log.

## On-call pay

You get an on-call allowance of ₹6,000 for each week on call, paid with salary. A call-out that lasts more than 1 hour also gives you time off in lieu equal to the time worked, for every grade. On-call employees who use their own phone get a mobile allowance of ₹1,000 a month, or a company phone.

## Limits

No one should work more than 48 hours a week on average over 17 weeks. Managers check overtime and call-outs every month. After a night call-out of more than 2 hours, start late the next day.
