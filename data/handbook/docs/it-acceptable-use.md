# Acceptable use of IT systems
Department: IT
Updated: 2026-02-02

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses Fernhill Software's laptops, phones, networks, accounts and cloud services, including contractors and visitors who use the guest Wi-Fi.

## Personal use

Reasonable personal use of company equipment is allowed, outside meetings and without affecting your work. Examples are reading the news, checking a personal email account in the browser, or paying a bill. Personal use must not cost the company money, use a lot of network capacity, or break any other policy.

## What is never allowed

- Accessing, storing or sharing illegal, offensive or sexually explicit material.
- Gambling, cryptocurrency mining or running a personal business on company equipment.
- Trying to get around security controls, such as the VPN, the web filter or the antivirus software.
- Accessing systems or data you have not been given access to, even if it is technically possible.
- Using personal cloud storage, personal email or personal messaging apps for company files.
- Connecting unknown USB drives found in public places.

## Email and chat

Write work messages as if they might be read by the person they are about. Do not forward company email to a personal address. Use company chat for work conversations. Customer data must not be shared in chat channels with external guests.

## Monitoring

The company monitors its systems to keep them secure. This includes logs of sign-ins, web traffic, file sharing and email metadata. The Security team may look at the content of company accounts only during an investigation approved by the Head of People and the Head of Security. Personal devices on the guest Wi-Fi are not monitored beyond the connection logs.

## Social media

You may say on social media that you work at Fernhill. Do not share confidential information, customer names or screenshots of internal systems. Make clear that your views are your own.

## Breaking this policy

Breaking this policy may lead to your access being removed and to disciplinary action. Report anything you see that breaks this policy to the Security team at security@fernhill.example.
