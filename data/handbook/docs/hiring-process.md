# Hiring process
Department: Talent
Updated: 2026-02-16

## Opening a role

Every new role needs an approved request in PeopleHub before the job is advertised. The department head and Finance must both approve the request. Replacing a person who is leaving also needs a request, but Finance approves it within 2 working days.

## The job advert

The Talent team writes the advert with the hiring manager. Adverts use plain language, list only the skills the job really needs, and give the salary range. Every advert stays open for at least 10 working days. Internal candidates may apply for any role after 12 months in their current role, and they are told about new roles a week before external candidates.

## The interview process

Every hire goes through the same three stages:
- A 30-minute screening call with the Talent team.
- A work sample or technical exercise that takes no more than 3 hours, and is paid if it takes longer than 1 hour.
- A final interview with a panel.

The interview panel has at least 3 people, including at least 1 person from another team. All panel members complete interview training before they join a panel. Each panel member writes their feedback in PeopleHub before talking to the others, so that one strong voice does not sway the rest.

## Speed

Candidates hear back within 2 working days after each stage. The offer is made within 5 working days of the final interview. Slow hiring loses good candidates, so the Talent team reports any delay to the hiring manager.

## Fair hiring

Decisions are based only on the skills and behaviours listed in the advert. Questions about family plans, religion, age or health are never asked. AI tools are not used to screen, score or reject candidates, as the AI tools policy says. Reasonable adjustments, such as extra time for the work sample, are offered to every candidate.

## Offers and checks

The Talent team makes every offer in writing. Offers are conditional on references and a background check, which take up to 10 working days. Hiring managers must not promise a salary or a start date before the offer letter.

## Referrals

Employees who refer a friend can earn a referral bonus, as the referral policy explains. Referred candidates go through exactly the same process.

## Data about candidates

Candidate data is Restricted. It is kept for 6 months after the role closes, then deleted, unless the candidate agrees to stay in the talent pool for 12 months.
