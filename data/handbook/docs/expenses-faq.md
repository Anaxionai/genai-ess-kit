# Expenses: frequently asked questions
Department: Finance
Updated: 2026-01-12

## How long do I have to submit a claim?

You have 30 days from the day you spent the money. After 90 days a claim is not paid at all. Submitting once a month is a good habit.

## When will I get my money?

If your manager approves the claim by the 20th of the month, it is paid with that month's salary on the last working day of the month. If it is approved later, it is paid the month after.

## I lost a receipt. What do I do?

Fill in a missing-receipt declaration in ExpenseHub, saying what you bought, when and for how much. You may use a declaration at most twice a year. After that, a cost without a receipt is not paid.

## Do I need a receipt for a small taxi fare?

No. Receipts are needed only for items over ₹500. Keep a receipt anyway when you can, because it makes your manager's check quicker.

## I paid in another currency. Which rate is used?

The rate on your card statement. Attach the statement line to the claim, with your personal details hidden.

## Can I claim a coffee while I work from home?

No. Food and drink while you work at home or in your own office are personal costs.

## Can I claim a meal when I work late?

Yes. If your manager asks you to work in the office after 20:00, you may claim a meal of up to ₹600 with a receipt.

## Who approves my claim when my manager is on leave?

Your manager's manager. ExpenseHub forwards the claim automatically after 5 working days.

## Can I claim for a client meal?

Yes, within the limits in the client meals policy. Claim it as a client meal, not as part of the daily allowance, and list who attended.

## My claim was returned. Why?

The most common reasons are a missing receipt, a cost above a limit, or a claim older than 30 days. The return note in ExpenseHub gives the reason. Correct it and submit it again within 10 working days.

## Can somebody else submit claims for me?

Yes. An assistant can prepare claims for you, but you must submit them yourself, because submitting confirms they are true.

## Are expense payments taxed?

No, as long as they repay real business costs. Allowances paid with salary, such as the internet allowance, are taxed as income.
