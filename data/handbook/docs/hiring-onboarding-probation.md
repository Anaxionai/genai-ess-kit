# Onboarding, probation and notice periods
Department: Talent
Updated: 2026-02-16

## Your first day

Your laptop, accounts and badge are ready on your first day. Your manager or your buddy meets you at reception at 10:00. Remote employees receive their laptop by courier before day one and join a welcome video call at 10:00.

## Your buddy

Every new employee has a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Your buddy answers everyday questions, shows you how things work, and meets you at least once a week for the first month.

## Your first weeks

- Complete the security training within your first 5 working days.
- Set up multi-factor authentication and the password manager on day one.
- Read this handbook in your first 2 weeks.
- Agree your goals for the first 3 months with your manager by the end of week two.

## Probation

Every new employee has a probation period of 6 months. Your manager holds a mid-point review at 3 months, and a final review before the end of the 6 months. The reviews look at the goals you agreed, and at how you work with others. If more time is needed, probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months. You are told in writing at least 2 weeks before your probation would have ended.

## Notice periods

During probation, the notice period is 2 weeks, on both sides. After probation, the notice period depends on your grade:
- Grades 1 to 3: 1 month.
- Grades 4 and 5: 2 months.
- Grade 6 and above: 3 months.

Give notice in writing to your manager and the People team. The company may pay you instead of asking you to work your notice.

## Leaving the company

Before your last day, hand over your work, return your laptop, badge and any corporate card, and match your final card payments. Unused annual leave is paid with your final salary. You will be invited to an exit conversation with the People team, which is optional and confidential.

## Feedback

New employees answer a short survey at 1 month and at 6 months. The answers help us improve onboarding for the next person.
