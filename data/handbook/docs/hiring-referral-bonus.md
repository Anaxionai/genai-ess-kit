# Referral bonus
Department: Talent
Updated: 2026-02-16

## The bonus

If you refer someone and we hire them, you receive a referral bonus of ₹75,000. For hard-to-fill roles marked as priority roles in PeopleHub, the bonus is ₹1,00,000. The bonus is paid with your salary, and it is taxed as income.

## When it is paid

The bonus is paid after your referred colleague passes their probation, which is 6 months after they start. Both of you must still be employed by Fernhill Software on that date, and neither of you may be working your notice. If the new colleague's probation is extended, the bonus waits until it ends.

## How to refer someone

Refer the person in PeopleHub with Refer a friend, before they apply. Attach their CV only with their permission. If the person applies first and you refer them afterwards, no bonus is paid. Only the first referral of a candidate counts.

## Who cannot receive a bonus

- The hiring manager for the role.
- Members of the interview panel for the role.
- Employees in the Talent and People teams.
- Directors and the leadership team.

Candidates already in the hiring process through a recruitment agency, or who applied to Fernhill in the previous 6 months, do not count as referrals.

## Rehiring former employees

Referring a former employee who left more than 12 months ago earns the normal bonus. Referring one who left less than 12 months ago earns half the bonus.

## Being fair

Referred candidates go through exactly the same hiring process as everyone else. Do not take part in decisions about someone you referred. Tell the Talent team if you know a candidate personally, even if you did not refer them.

## Limits

There is no limit on the number of referral bonuses you can earn. The Talent team publishes the list of open roles every Monday in the company chat.

## Questions

Ask the Talent team at talent@fernhill.example. They can tell you the status of your referral at any time.
