# Remote work guidelines (2024)
Department: People
Updated: 2024-03-01

## Status of this document

SUPERSEDED. This document was replaced on 1 January 2026 by the hybrid working policy. Do not follow these rules. They are kept for one year so that you can see what changed.

## Days in the office

Employees were expected in the office 3 days a week, chosen with their manager. There were no team anchor days, and desks were not booked in advance. Managers could agree 2 days a week for employees who lived more than 50 kilometres from the office.

## Working from home

Employees were asked to work from a quiet place with a reliable internet connection, and to use the VPN for all company systems. Employees had to be online from 09:30 to 17:30 on days they worked from home.

## Allowances

A home working allowance of ₹1,000 a month was paid with salary to employees who worked from home at least 1 day a week. Equipment could be borrowed from the office with the IT service desk's agreement. There was no allowance for buying home office furniture.

## Working abroad

Working from another country was not allowed, except on approved business travel. Employees who wanted to spend time abroad had to take annual leave or unpaid leave.

## Why it changed

The 2025 staff survey asked for fewer fixed office days and more flexibility. The hybrid working policy of 2026 reduced the office days to 2, added team anchor days and DeskBook, raised the internet allowance and introduced the work from anywhere policy.

## Equipment and security in 2024

Employees took their company laptop home every day and were asked to keep it in a locked drawer in the office overnight when they did not. Personal computers could be used for email in a web browser, but not for company files. Lost laptops had to be reported within 24 hours. These rules were also replaced: today a lost device must be reported within 1 hour, and personal computers may not be used for company work at all.
