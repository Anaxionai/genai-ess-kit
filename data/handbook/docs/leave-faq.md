# Leave: frequently asked questions
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## How many days of holiday do I get?

A full-time employee gets 25 days of annual leave a year, plus public holidays. After 3 years of service you get 26 days, and after 5 years you get 27 days. Part-time employees get the same pro rata. The annual leave policy has the full rules.

## Can I buy or sell leave?

You can buy up to 3 extra days of leave a year in January's benefits window in PeopleHub. The cost is the same as your daily pay, and it is taken from your salary in equal parts over the year. You cannot sell leave you do not use. You can carry up to 5 days into the next year, and they must be used by 31 March.

## I was ill during my holiday. Can I get the days back?

Yes, if you tell your manager on the first day you are ill and send a doctor's note for those days. The days then become sick leave, and your annual leave is given back. Without a doctor's note the days stay as annual leave.

## Do I need to book leave for a medical appointment?

Short appointments can be made up by working flexibly outside the core hours of 10:00 to 16:00. A half day or a full day away needs annual leave, or sick leave if you are unwell. Appointments related to pregnancy are paid time off and need no leave.

## Is a wellbeing day the same as annual leave?

No. Wellbeing days are a separate allowance of 2 days a year, with their own rules. They do not carry over and are not paid out when you leave.

## Who approves my leave when my manager is away?

Your manager's manager. PeopleHub sends the request on automatically after 3 working days without an answer.

## How much notice do I need to give?

For more than 3 days in a row, at least 10 working days. For 1 to 3 days, give as much notice as you reasonably can. Blocks longer than 15 working days also need your director's approval.

## Can my manager cancel leave that was already approved?

Only in an emergency, such as a serious incident that needs your skills. If a manager cancels approved leave, the company pays any costs you cannot get back, such as a non-refundable booking, after you claim them in ExpenseHub with receipts.

## What happens to my leave when I go on parental leave?

You keep earning annual leave during parental leave. The People team agrees with you when to take it, and the 31 March limit for carried-over days can be extended.

## Can I take leave during my notice period?

Yes, with your manager's approval. Unused leave is paid with your final salary, so you do not lose it.
