# Laptops and equipment
Department: IT
Updated: 2026-02-02

## Your laptop

Every employee gets a company laptop on their first day. The standard laptop has 16 GB of memory and a 512 GB drive. Engineers and data analysts may ask for a laptop with 32 GB of memory, with their manager's approval. Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if a laptop fails and cannot be repaired. The IT service desk contacts you about 4 weeks before your replacement is due.

## Setting it up

The laptop arrives with full-disk encryption, the VPN, the password manager and the approved software already installed. Sign in with your company account and set up multi-factor authentication on day one, as the passwords policy explains. Do not remove or turn off the security software.

## Looking after it

Lock the screen whenever you step away, with Windows key and L. The screen also locks by itself after 5 minutes. Never leave the laptop in a car, even in the boot. Carry it in a padded bag. Keep food and drink away from the keyboard. Restart the laptop at least once a week so updates can install. Updates that are waiting for more than 7 days are installed for you at the next restart.

## Personal use

Reasonable personal use is allowed, such as reading the news at lunch or paying a bill. The acceptable use policy explains the limits. Do not install personal software or store personal photos and files on the laptop, because the laptop can be wiped at any time to protect company data.

## Other equipment

Monitors, keyboards, mice and headsets are available on loan from the IT service desk. Docking stations are on every office desk. Phones are given only to employees on the on-call rota who ask for one.

## Repairs and faults

Report a fault through the IT service desk portal, or call extension 4400. A loan laptop is given the same day while yours is repaired. Do not take the laptop to a repair shop yourself.

## Lost or stolen laptops

Report a lost or stolen laptop within 1 hour, as the lost devices policy explains.

## Leaving the company

Return the laptop, its charger and any borrowed equipment on your last day. Remote employees receive a prepaid courier box and must send everything back within 5 working days. Equipment not returned is deducted from your final pay at its current value.
