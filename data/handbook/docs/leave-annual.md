# Annual leave
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to every employee of Fernhill Software, full time and part time. Contractors agree their time off in their own contract and are not covered here. It replaces all earlier versions of the annual leave policy. Where this policy and a manager's email disagree, this policy wins.

## How much leave you get

Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave in each leave year. Public holidays are on top of the 25 days. Part-time employees get leave pro rata: someone who works three days a week gets 15 days. Long service adds leave. After 3 years of service you get 1 extra day, and after 5 years you get 2 extra days, so the most anyone gets is 27 days. The extra days start on the first 1 January after your anniversary. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. If you join or leave during the year, you get leave pro rata for the months you work, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## Booking leave

Request every day of leave in PeopleHub before you take it. Your manager approves or declines a request within 3 working days. If your manager is away, the request goes to their manager after 3 working days. For more than 3 consecutive working days, request the leave at least 10 working days before the first day. For shorter leave, give as much notice as you can. You may take up to 15 consecutive working days at once. A longer block needs your director's approval as well as your manager's. Managers may decline a request when too many people in a small team are away at the same time, and they must say why in PeopleHub.

## Carrying leave over

You may carry up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the new year, or they are lost. Days above the limit of 5 are lost on 31 December. Nobody is paid for days that are lost. If you could not take your leave because of long sickness or parental leave, the People team agrees a longer carry-over with you in writing. Plan your leave early in the year, because a rush of requests in December is often declined.

## The end-of-year closure

Both offices close from 24 December to 1 January. The public holidays in that period are free, and the other working days come out of your annual leave. In 2026 that is 3 days, so keep 3 days back for the closure. Details are in the public holidays policy.

## Leaving the company

When you leave, unused annual leave for the current year is paid with your final salary. Leave you have taken beyond what you have earned by your last day is deducted from your final pay. Carried-over days that have not expired are paid as well.

## Questions

Ask your manager first, then the People team at people@fernhill.example. The leave FAQ answers the most common questions, such as buying extra days.
