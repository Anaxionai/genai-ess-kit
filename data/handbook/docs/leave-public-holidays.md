# Public holidays and the end-of-year closure
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Public holidays

Each office observes 10 public holidays a year. The list of dates for each office is published in PeopleHub every December for the next year. The Riverside and Hilltop offices may have slightly different lists. If you work from the other office's city for a week or more, you follow that office's holidays for that time. Fully remote employees follow the list of the office named in their contract.

## Working on a public holiday

Nobody is expected to work on a public holiday unless their manager asks in advance, or they are on the on-call rota. If your manager asks you to work on a public holiday, you get a day in lieu, which you take within 3 months. Employees in grades 1 to 3 may choose double pay for the hours worked instead of a day in lieu. Record the choice in PeopleHub before the end of the month.

## Holidays that fall on a weekend

If a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, there is no extra day off unless the published list says so.

## The end-of-year closure

Both offices close from 24 December to 1 January. Public holidays in that period are free. The other working days in that period come out of your annual leave. In 2026 that is 3 days, so keep 3 days of annual leave for the closure. New starters who have not earned enough leave by December may take those days as unpaid leave instead, or work from home if their manager agrees.

## On call during the closure

The on-call rota continues during the closure. Employees on call during the closure get the normal on-call allowance and do not use annual leave for the days they cover. They also get a day in lieu for every public holiday they cover.

## Religious and cultural festivals

Many festivals are not on the public holiday list. You may use annual leave for them, and managers should approve those requests unless there is a strong business reason. You may also swap a public holiday you do not observe for another day, with your manager's agreement, once a year.
