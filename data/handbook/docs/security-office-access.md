# Office access and visitors
Department: Security
Updated: 2026-02-02

## Opening hours

The Riverside office is open from 07:00 to 20:00 on working days. The Hilltop office is open from 07:30 to 19:00 on working days. Weekend and public holiday access needs approval from the Facilities team, requested in DeskBook at least 2 working days before.

## Your badge

Your badge opens the doors of both offices. Wear it where it can be seen while you are in the office. Never lend your badge, and never let someone follow you through a door without their own badge, even if you know them. If a person without a badge asks to be let in, send them to reception politely. A lost badge is reported within 1 hour, as the lost devices policy explains.

## Visitors

Register every visitor in the reception system at least 1 working day before the visit. Visitors sign in at reception, receive a visitor badge, and are escorted by their host at all times. Visitors who will enter an area where Restricted data is handled, such as the payroll room, must sign a non-disclosure agreement at reception first. Visitors may use the guest Wi-Fi, never the staff network. Contractors working in the office for more than 2 weeks get a contractor badge from Facilities.

## Leaving your desk

At the end of each day, clear your desk of papers, lock away anything Confidential or Restricted, and take your laptop home or lock it in your locker. Lock your screen every time you leave your desk. Shred papers you no longer need in the locked shredding bins.

## Parking

The Riverside office has 40 parking spaces and the Hilltop office has 15. Book a space in DeskBook, at most 2 weeks ahead. Spaces for employees with accessibility needs are kept free and cannot be booked by others. The 4 electric vehicle chargers at Riverside may be used for up to 4 hours at a time.

## Emergencies

Fire drills take place twice a year. When the alarm sounds, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point in the car park. Do not stop for belongings. Each floor has trained first aiders and fire wardens, named on the notice board next to the lifts.

## Cameras

Cameras cover entrances, the car park and reception. Footage is kept for 30 days and viewed only by the Security team during an investigation.
