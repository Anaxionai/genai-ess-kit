# Lost or stolen devices
Department: IT
Updated: 2026-02-02

## Report it within 1 hour

If a company laptop, phone, security key or access badge is lost or stolen, report it within 1 hour of noticing. Call the IT service desk on extension 4400, or from outside the office on the number printed on the back of your badge. Out of hours, the call goes to the on-call IT engineer. If you cannot call, email it-help@fernhill.example from any device.

## What IT does

The IT service desk locks the device and signs it out of every company account at once. If the device is not found within 24 hours, it is wiped remotely. Your badge is cancelled and a new one is printed for you. Because the laptop is encrypted, a thief cannot read its files, but reporting quickly still matters, because accounts that are signed in can be used.

## Theft

If the device was stolen, report the theft to the police within 24 hours and send the report number to the IT service desk. For a theft abroad, also tell the travel insurance assistance line, as the international travel policy explains.

## Getting a replacement

A replacement laptop is ready within 2 working days. Your files are restored from the company cloud storage, so keep your work there and not only on the laptop. A replacement phone for on-call employees is ready within 1 working day.

## Personal phones with work apps

If you use work email or chat on a personal phone and lose it, report it the same way. IT removes the work apps and their data from the phone, but does not touch your personal data.

## Will I have to pay?

No, unless the loss happened because you clearly broke this handbook, for example by leaving the laptop visible in a parked car. In that case the company may ask you to pay part of the cost, up to ₹20,000.

## Finding it again

If you find the device after reporting it, tell the IT service desk before you switch it on. It may need to be checked before you use it again.

## Security incidents

A lost or stolen device is also a security incident. The IT service desk passes every report to the Security team, so you do not need to report it twice.
