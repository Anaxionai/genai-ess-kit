# Unpaid leave
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## What unpaid leave is for

Unpaid leave gives you time away from work when your paid leave is not enough. Common reasons are a family event abroad, a long trip, moving house, caring for a relative, or study. You do not need to give a detailed reason, but your manager needs enough information to plan your work.

## How much you can take

You may ask for up to 20 working days of unpaid leave in a calendar year. Longer periods are not offered. Use your annual leave first, unless the People team agrees otherwise, for example when you want to keep leave for a planned family event.

## How to ask

Request unpaid leave in PeopleHub at least 4 weeks before it starts, or as soon as you can in an emergency. Your manager and the People team must both approve it. They reply within 5 working days. A request can be declined if the work cannot be covered, and the reason is given in writing.

## Effect on pay and benefits

Unpaid days reduce your salary for that month. Each unpaid day reduces your pay by one working day's pay. Unpaid days also reduce your annual leave for the year pro rata: 20 unpaid days reduce it by about 2 days. Your health insurance continues as normal. Retirement plan contributions are based on the pay you actually receive that month. Unpaid leave does not break your length of service.

## While you are away

Set an out-of-office message that gives a colleague's name. Hand over your open work before you go, and agree with your manager whether you want to be contacted in an emergency. Return your company laptop to IT only if you will be away for more than 15 working days and will not use it.

## Coming back

You return to your job on the same terms. If you cannot return on the agreed date, tell your manager at once. Days beyond the agreed period without approval are treated as unauthorised absence.

## Emergencies

In a real emergency, such as a family member taken suddenly ill, call your manager. Short emergency time off for dependants is agreed on the spot and recorded afterwards.
