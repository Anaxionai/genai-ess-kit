# Performance reviews and pay reviews
Department: People
Updated: 2026-01-05

## Performance reviews

Everyone has a performance review twice a year, in March and in September. Before each review, you write a short self-review in PeopleHub, and you may ask up to 3 colleagues for written feedback. Your manager writes their view, and you meet for at least 45 minutes to discuss it. Reviews look at what you achieved against your goals and at how you worked with others.

## Ratings

Each review gives a rating from 1 to 4:
- 1 means not yet meeting expectations.
- 2 means meeting most expectations.
- 3 means meeting all expectations.
- 4 means far exceeding expectations.

Ratings are checked across teams by a group of managers, so that the same work gets the same rating everywhere. AI tools are never used to set ratings.

## If you disagree

If you disagree with your rating, write to the People team within 10 working days of the review. A manager from another department reviews it and meets you. Their decision is final.

## Pay reviews

Pay is reviewed once a year. Increases take effect on 1 April and are based on your role's market rate, your rating from the September review and the March review, and the company's results. You receive a letter in March with your new pay. Pay is never discussed by managers in public channels.

## Promotions

Promotions are considered in the September cycle. Your manager proposes a promotion with evidence, and a panel of managers decides. A promotion comes with a pay increase of at least 8%, effective on 1 October.

## Bonus

The company bonus is paid in May if the company meets its yearly goals. Everyone who has passed probation gets the same percentage of salary, so that the bonus rewards shared results.

## Support to improve

If you receive a rating of 1, your manager agrees a support plan with you, with clear goals and regular check-ins over 3 months. Training from your learning budget can be part of the plan.
